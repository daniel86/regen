Detailed Description

OpenGL Objects that contain images.

RenderBuffer's are created and used specifically with Framebuffer Objects, and are optimized for being used as render targets, while Textures may not be. RBOs cannot be directly accessed from shaders, and they do not support mipmapping or texture filtering.
